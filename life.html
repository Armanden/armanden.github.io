Life

you open your eyes to a world of colours

breathe a slither of wonders

looking around, so curious

walking on half steps, to find us

dreaming away on late nights,

when sun sets and moon rises

creating on thin air, drawing on empty canvas

sharing such on joy and heartness

traveling doing thus,

sometimes on exhaust some on lightness

never stopping on pursuit once

drifting on lakes, as sky falls down upon thus

merging with clouds when time comes to us
